Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying. Can't find the answer? Reach us anytime.

What is the duration of the B.Tech CSE program?
+

The B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering is a 4-year (8-semester) full-time undergraduate program. Students can opt for a fast-track option allowing completion in 3.5 years through summer semesters.

What are the admission requirements?
+

Candidates must have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a minimum aggregate of 60%. A valid JEE Main score is required for domestic students. International students may apply with equivalent qualifications and SAT/ACT scores.

Is a scholarship available for this program?
+

Yes, merit-based scholarships covering up to 100% of tuition fees are available for top performers. Need-based financial aid is also offered. All students are eligible to apply for government scholarships and education loans through our partnered banks.

What specialisations are available?
+

Students can specialise in one of six tracks: Artificial Intelligence & ML, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Software Systems, or IoT & Embedded Systems. Specialisations begin from Semester 5.

Are hostel facilities available on campus?
+

Yes, we have fully furnished separate hostels for male and female students with 24/7 Wi-Fi, dining, recreation rooms, and on-campus medical assistance. Allocation is first-come first-served, with priority for outstation students.

What is the placement record for CSE graduates?
+

Our placement rate stands at 98% with over 250 recruiting companies visiting campus annually. The highest package in 2023–24 was ₹62 LPA (domestic) and the average package was ₹24 LPA.

Can I pursue higher studies after graduation?
+

Absolutely. Many graduates go on to pursue M.Tech, MBA, or MS programs at MIT, Stanford, IITs, and IIMs. We have dedicated preparation programs for GRE, GMAT, and GATE exams.

Are the AWS and Google certifications genuinely included?
+

Yes — exam vouchers and preparatory training for both AWS Cloud Practitioner and Google Associate Cloud Engineer are fully included in your program fees. Training is conducted in Semesters 5 and 6 by certified instructors.

Is the program recognised internationally?
+

Our B.Tech program is NAAC A++ accredited and NBA approved. We have academic partnerships with 40+ universities worldwide including UC Berkeley, TU Munich, and NUS Singapore. Our degrees are recognised globally by employers and institutions.

How do I apply and what is the deadline?
+

Applications are submitted online through our portal — fill the form, upload documents, and schedule a counselling session. For the 2025–26 batch, the last date is 30 June 2025. Early applicants receive priority in scholarship allocation and hostel assignment.
