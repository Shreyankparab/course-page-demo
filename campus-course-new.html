Admission Process

Follow these step-by-step guidelines to secure your admission, whether you are a domestic or international applicant.

  • Register yourself for B.Des in Communication Design - Visual Communication by clicking here on our Online Application Platform.
  • Fill in all information correctly and accurately as required in the Online Application Platform.
  • Upload the scanned copy of the following documents:
    • Original 10th Mark sheet
    • Original 12th or Equivalent Mark sheet
    • 1 photo Id proof
    • 1 Address proof
    • 1 passport sized photograph
  • Make a payment of Rs. 500 (non-refundable) against STAC (Seamedu Technical Assessment and Counseling). After completing the above steps, you will receive a confirmation of your application being submitted using your registered email ID.

Within 15 days of application of admission, you will be intimated on your selection (if selected) B.Des in Communication Design - Visual Communication by email on your registered email id. On receipt of the email, please complete the following:

  • Please sign in to the Seamedu Online Application Platform using your registered username and password.
  • Please respond to any additional queries that arise.
  • Make payment as described by the counselor.
  • On receipt of the same, Provisional Admission letter will be issued for the course.

Submit the filled-in Final Admission form and copies of the following documents.

  • Two photo copies of 10th Mark sheet
  • Two photo copies of 12th or Equivalent Mark sheet
  • Two photo copies of photo Id proof
  • Two photocopies of Address proof
  • Six passport sized photographs with red background

Note: Please carry the Original documents for Verification. You’ll get all your original documents back.

  • Register yourself for B.Des in Communication Design - Visual Communication by clicking here on our Online Application Platform.
  • Fill in all information correctly and accurately as required in the Online Application Platform.
  • Upload the scanned copy of the following documents:
    • Valid Passport
    • Marks transcripts of:
      • High school
      • Bachelor’s Degree (if applicable)
      • Previous VISA to India if any
  • Make a payment of $50 (non-refundable) against STAC (Seamedu Technical Assessment and Counseling). After completing the above steps, you will receive a confirmation of your application being submitted using your registered email ID.

Within 15 days of your application, you will be intimated about your selection (if selected) B.Des in Communication Design - Visual Communication by email on your registered email ID. On receipt of the email, please complete the following process:

  • Please sign in to the Seamedu Online Application Platform using your registered username and password.
  • Complete the set of steps as prompted
  • Make a payment of $160 as processing and eligibility fees and $350 as admission confirmation fee [We adjust the admission confirmation fee against the total fees.] *Both fee amounts are non-refundable

On receipt of the Provisional Admission Letter, please complete the following process:

  • Have all Marks Transcripts attested by the Ministry of Higher Education in your country
  • Apply for visa at the nearest Indian mission or Indian Embassy in your country (You must take an endorsed Student Visa)
  • Email us a copy of the attested transcripts and the visa issued

On arrival in India, you must report to the International Students’ Cell to take admission in person

Fill in and submit the Final Admission Form along with copies of the following documents:

  • Endorsed Student Visa
  • Medical Fitness Report / Yellow Fever Vaccination Card
  • C-Form and House Agreement
  • 4 Photographs
  • Complete Payment of Fee / Receipts

Please carry all your original documents for verification. You’ll get all your original documents back after post-verification. Additional important notes:

  • All foreign students must take the English Language Proficiency Test. Clearance of this test is a must for firm admission.
  • You need to report to the FRO for registration. Students must register within 14 days of arrival in India or they may have to pay late fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying. Can't find the answer? Reach us anytime.

What is the duration of the B.Tech CSE program?
+

The B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering is a 4-year (8-semester) full-time undergraduate program. Students can opt for a fast-track option allowing completion in 3.5 years through summer semesters.

What are the admission requirements?
+

Candidates must have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a minimum aggregate of 60%. A valid JEE Main score is required for domestic students. International students may apply with equivalent qualifications and SAT/ACT scores.

Is a scholarship available for this program?
+

Yes, merit-based scholarships covering up to 100% of tuition fees are available for top performers. Need-based financial aid is also offered. All students are eligible to apply for government scholarships and education loans through our partnered banks.

What specialisations are available?
+

Students can specialise in one of six tracks: Artificial Intelligence & ML, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Software Systems, or IoT & Embedded Systems. Specialisations begin from Semester 5.

Are hostel facilities available on campus?
+

Yes, we have fully furnished separate hostels for male and female students with 24/7 Wi-Fi, dining, recreation rooms, and on-campus medical assistance. Allocation is first-come first-served, with priority for outstation students.

What is the placement record for CSE graduates?
+

Our placement rate stands at 98% with over 250 recruiting companies visiting campus annually. The highest package in 2023–24 was ₹62 LPA (domestic) and the average package was ₹24 LPA.

Can I pursue higher studies after graduation?
+

Absolutely. Many graduates go on to pursue M.Tech, MBA, or MS programs at MIT, Stanford, IITs, and IIMs. We have dedicated preparation programs for GRE, GMAT, and GATE exams.

Are the AWS and Google certifications genuinely included?
+

Yes — exam vouchers and preparatory training for both AWS Cloud Practitioner and Google Associate Cloud Engineer are fully included in your program fees. Training is conducted in Semesters 5 and 6 by certified instructors.

Is the program recognised internationally?
+

Our B.Tech program is NAAC A++ accredited and NBA approved. We have academic partnerships with 40+ universities worldwide including UC Berkeley, TU Munich, and NUS Singapore. Our degrees are recognised globally by employers and institutions.

How do I apply and what is the deadline?
+

Applications are submitted online through our portal — fill the form, upload documents, and schedule a counselling session. For the 2025–26 batch, the last date is 30 June 2025. Early applicants receive priority in scholarship allocation and hostel assignment.
