Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying. Can't find the answer? Reach us anytime.

What courses does Seamedu offer?
+

Seamedu offers a wide range of industry-focused programs in Media & Entertainment, including Sound Engineering, Film Making, Photography, Game Design, Game Development, Animation, VFX, Media Management and Journalism.

Where are Seamedu's campuses located?
+

Seamedu has campuses in Pune, Gurugram, and Kolkata. Affiliated with Ajeenkya D Y Patil University (ADPYU - Pune), D Y Patil University (Ambi - Pune), The NorthCap University (NCU - Gurugram), and Techno India University (TIU - Kolkata).

What is the minimum qualification to apply?
+

For undergraduate (UG) programs, the minimum qualification is completion of Class 12 (or equivalent) in any stream from a recognized board. For postgraduate (PG) programs, a bachelor's degree in any discipline is required.

How do I apply to Seamedu?
+

You can apply online through our official website by clicking the 'Apply Now' button, filling in the application form, uploading the required documents, and paying the application fee.

What is the fee structure? Are scholarships available?
+

The fee structure varies by course and campus. Detailed information can be found in our prospectus or obtained by connecting with our admissions team. Scholarships are available based on merit and specific eligibility criteria.

Who will teach me at Seamedu?
+

Our faculty consists of experienced academicians and seasoned industry professionals who bring real-world insights, practical knowledge, and mentorship to the classroom.

What facilities does Seamedu offer?
+

Seamedu offers state-of-the-art facilities, including professional recording studios, film editing suites, photography labs, high-end computer labs with industry-standard software, and modern classrooms.

What are the career outcomes for Seamedu graduates?
+

Seamedu has a dedicated placement cell that actively connects students with leading companies across their respective industries. Our graduates have gone on to work with some of the biggest names in media, technology, management, design and hospitality.

What makes Seamedu different from other colleges?
+

Seamedu's strength lies in its industry-integrated curriculum, professional infrastructure, hands-on learning from Year 1, and strong placement support. Students graduate with real skills and a portfolio that helps them in the real world.

Are internships part of the curriculum?
+

Yes, internships are an integral part of Seamedu's curriculum. Students are encouraged and assisted by our placement cell to take up industry internships during their course, helping them gain hands-on professional experience before graduation.

Established in 2008, Seamedu is a leading media school offering industry-integrated education in Sound Engineering, Film Making, VFX, and Game Design.
